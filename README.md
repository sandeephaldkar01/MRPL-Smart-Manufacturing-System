MRPL Smart Manufacturing System

A web-based Manufacturing and Quality Management System designed to manage manufacturing operations, raw materials, production, quality control, inventory, employees, attendance, orders, and dispatch.

Built using Python, Streamlit, and SQLite, the application provides a centralized platform with role-based access for Admin, Manager, and Employee users.

🚀 Features

📊 Management Dashboard

- Overview of raw materials, production batches, finished goods, orders, and dispatches.
- Production and order status monitoring.
- Low-stock alerts based on minimum stock levels.

🧱 Raw Material Management

- Add and manage raw material records.
- Track current stock and minimum stock levels.
- Identify materials requiring restocking.

🏭 Production Management

- Manage production batches.
- Record planned and actual production quantities.
- Monitor production status.

🧪 Quality Control

- Maintain quality inspection records.
- Track quality status and Pass/Fail results.
- Monitor quality-control information for manufactured products.

📦 Finished Goods Management

- Maintain finished product records.
- Track product quantities, stock, and status.

🚚 Orders & Dispatch Management

- Manage customer orders and order status.
- Maintain dispatch records.
- Record dispatch date, vehicle number, driver name, and dispatched quantity.

👥 Employee Management

- Maintain employee profiles and employment information.
- Manage employee codes, departments, designations, contact details, joining dates, and status.

🕐 Attendance Management

- Record employee attendance.
- Maintain check-in and check-out times.
- Support Present, Absent, and Leave statuses.
- Allow employees to view their attendance and mark today's check-in.

📅 Leave & Task Management

- Submit and track employee leave requests.
- Support Sick Leave, Casual Leave, and Earned Leave.
- Track Pending, Approved, and Rejected leave requests.
- Allow employees to view assigned tasks.

🛍️ Product Catalogue

The application includes a public product catalogue for refractory products.

Shaped Refractory Products

- Fireclay & High Alumina Bricks
- Pre-Cast Pre-Fired (PCPF) Blocks
- Silicon Carbide Bricks & Shapes
- Acid-resistant Bricks

Unshaped Refractory Products

- Dense Castables
- Low & Ultra Low Cement Castables
- Insulating Castables
- Plastic Masses
- High Alumina Cement & Binder
- Grouting Materials
- Mortars
- Gunning Mixes

The catalogue covers 12 product types and can include product descriptions, images, categories, and links to official product information.

🔐 Authentication & Role-Based Access

The system supports three user roles:

- Admin: Access to all management modules, including user management.
- Manager: Access to operational and employee management modules.
- Employee: Access to personal profile, attendance, leave requests, and assigned tasks.

🙍 Employee Self-Service

Employees can access their personal information, attendance records, leave requests, and assigned tasks through dedicated sections.

🛠️ Technology Stack

- Programming Language: Python
- Web Framework: Streamlit
- Database: SQLite
- Database File: "mrpl.db"

🗄️ Database

The application uses SQLite to store and manage operational records, including raw materials, production, quality control, finished goods, orders, dispatches, employees, attendance, leave, tasks, products, and users.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of the MRPL Smart Manufacturing System is to bring essential manufacturing and administrative activities into a centralized web application, improve access to operational information, and support inventory tracking, production monitoring, quality management, and employee self-service.
